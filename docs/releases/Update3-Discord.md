# Dynamic Gameday Atmosphere — Update 3

**Rebuilt attendance**
- New peak crowds based on school support, AP rank, season success and matchup demand, calibrated against real attendance trends.
- Openers, night games, senior day, in-state games and service-academy matchups can draw more fans. Smaller schools can have big crowds when the circumstances fit.
- Better arrivals, halftime returns and late departures. Utah joins the late-arriving schools. More natural seating gaps, with desirable seats staying fuller.

**New crowd sound**
- Sparse crowds stay quieter without losing their big-play reactions. Larger packed crowds can reach +30% audio signal over vanilla.
- 80 listening-tested sounds added as variety for existing reactions. More cheers, boos, disappointment and anticipation. No separate chant cut or old excitement limiter.

**Better shake and broadcast**
- Shake follows occupied seats, with stronger small-stadium effects, smoother snap fades and run-to-TD transitions.
- Improved defensive reactions and overtime, including stops, kicks and two-point tries. Earned final-play effects can finish into postgame.
- Mode-aware replay handling: full-screen then inset requests in manual play; full-screen without an added inset in Slow Sim. Native presentation still controls what appears.

**Settings, 2020 Mode and fixes**
- New Gameday Atmosphere settings tab with plain-English options, plus pause-menu fullness.
- Optional 2020 Mode: empty stands, muted collective crowd/bands/drums/PA and a more open stadium sound.
- Post-whistle hitch fix, saved-crowd fixes, fewer stale/duplicate reactions and clearer logs.
- Updated for the October 30 Windows game update.

**Close the game and MMC before updating. Old DGA settings reset once with an automatic backup—no INI deletion needed.**
Offline Dynasty is the tested mode. Full details are in the included feature list.
