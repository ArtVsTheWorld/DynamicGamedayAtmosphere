# Update 3 - candidate

Working build **1.80.0**. Not published; targeted live acceptance remains pending. Update 2 (1.54.0) remains the latest public release.

- **In-game settings:** A Gameday Atmosphere tab after Audio Settings, 35 essential controls across Attendance, Soundscape and Broadcast (10/12/13), toggles before numeric controls, no troubleshooting section, simpler option names/help, and Stadium Fullness beside Resume in the pause menu.
- **Smoother sound and shake:** Live runs, catches, returns, touchdowns and eruptions hand off without restarting a full-size swell. Added movement is paired with crowd sound, follows appropriate live camera cuts, respects Dynamic Camera Effects and stays out of recorded replay.
- **Sparse native-camera filtering:** The first HFA event on a newly created live camera is filtered immediately, including before its event row exists. Compatible broadcast/replay camera classes are skipped without disabling the filter for supported live views. The native 50% fullness cutoff remains separate from DGA's layered-atmosphere requirements; a below-layered game can still have native camera shake above that cutoff.
- **Gentler pre-snap motion:** Pre-punt buildup is 50% softer; home-offense critical-down buildup is 15% softer. Both retain smooth fades and the configured strength controls.
- **Split-crowd camera strength:** Crowd-driven shake uses 75% of normal strength for either team at neutral venues. Each team can receive its first-Q1-offensive-play surge once. The snap fade follows the new live camera; pause-menu fullness uses an en dash.
- **Score motion handoff:** An existing run/catch shake builds into TD/eruption strength over 300 ms, preserving its oscillator and avoiding another impact. Isolated scores keep their configured onset.
- **Candidate replay sequence:** In verified manual play, confirmed TD/takeaway routes hold the inset until the native full-screen clip sequence ends, then request a separate repeat. Slow Sim keeps native full-screen playback without an added inset request or missing-inset timeout, and mode switches cancel pending inset work. An active native replay blocks timeout fallback. Live checks remain required; native recording availability and postgame highlight variety are not guaranteed. Kneels/spikes do not receive DGA routine replay promotion.
- **Cheer diagnostics and recovery:** Queue dispatch and native voice registration have separate logs. A silently dropped cheer gets at most one short same-play recovery; native voice/volume limits remain.
- **Sack classification:** A verified released forward pass blocks a carried-over native sack flag.
- **Deep-pass anticipation:** A fast supporting swell while either offense's verified deep throw is airborne, with no pre-catch cheer/shake or extra volume bonus.
- **More precise reactions:** Gentler skill buildup, a cheer for hurdles, big-hit crowd sound for trucks, and small home-defense third/fourth-down breakup/knockout reactions. Regular broken tackles, successful spins and successful jukes are sound-only. Confirmed turnover-on-downs stops also get a small paired shake/cheer. Late native knockout/deflection proof is tracked without treating an ordinary incompletion as a breakup. Hit-stick shake requires a confirmed third/fourth-down stop. Kneels and clock-stopping spikes are excluded from added buildup and sack/stop reactions. Away takeaways clear previous home-positive shake immediately. Delayed forced-fumble detection tracks the original carrier; recovery alone no longer adds shake.
- **Sharper forced-fumble onset:** Confirmed forces use a 35 ms attack and at most a 50 ms handoff into the impact. Strength, duration, sound and ownership checks remain the same.
- **Sparse crowds keep their reaction peaks:** Calmer background sound remains, but native big-play peaks can rise above the bed ceiling. The ceiling blends with empty seats and strength instead of switching fully on just below 50%. Crowd scaling and volume limits remain.
- **Quieter games still react:** Verified plays can receive a single native cheer or disappointment sound below the atmosphere gate, with softer skill/pass swells. Shake, fuller roar layers, follow-up cheers, eruptions and volume bonuses keep their existing requirements.
- **Better game stories:** Upset Watch sound for credible home bids against Top 25 visitors and faster beat-the-traffic departures in a home blowout: the last third of Q4 for 17+ deficits, with a gentle last-half start for 28+. Existing packed-house requirements, volume caps and crowd floors remain.
- **Rainy home blowouts:** In the last half of Q4, a home lead above 21 gradually weakens winning-fan protection, reaching full severity at 35+. Scores raise the existing departure target; no fixed crowd deduction occurs on the scoring play. A separate, non-stacking exception permits up to 10 extra original-crowd points above the normal quarter cap (less with a low custom cap), with hard crowd floors intact. Dry games, close games, earlier quarters, neutral venues and postseason rules retain their existing behavior.
- **More selective eruptions:** The opening-touchdown exception covers only the game's first two tracked plays, including kickoff. A 60+ yard close-game touchdown gets a modest standard-TD accent; decisive winning plays can still erupt.
- **Stadium cannons:** Their own strength control, default 15%, using the shared route for all nine native variants. Normal logs report detected blasts once, with sequence, variant and strength.
- **Clearer troubleshooting:** Exact activation failure stages, throttled retries, organized plugin-owned files, controlled previews and read-only compatibility reports.

**Updating resets old DGA INI values once.** The original is backed up automatically under `Plugins/DynamicGamedayAtmosphere/Backups`. Current defaults replace the old values; reapply desired settings afterward. Later launches and routine updates preserve those new settings. Close MMC and the game before replacing the plugin DLL, then restart both.

Offline Dynasty is the verified mode. Other modes, every cannon venue and every rare play are not implied to have been tested. See the [current checklist](../DIAGNOSTICS.md) for the distinction between automated coverage, observed game signals and confirmed live behavior.
